Rayfield Gen2 [Better]

Devlog 1 • July 24, 2026

Rayfield Gen2 [Better] is the official Rayfield Gen2 by Sirius, loaded completely unchanged, with two upgrades applied automatically to every window. Same library, same look, same API, it just fixes the two things the official Gen2 gets wrong. Nothing else is touched.

Improvements:
Window resizing. The official Gen2 window is a fixed size with no way to resize it. Better adds a smooth corner grip that stretches the window to follow your cursor, with a minimum size so the top bar never breaks.
Better dropdowns. The official Gen2 opens a dropdown to a tiny sliver when it only holds a couple of options, and it slices the rounded corners off the dropdown border. Better opens every dropdown to a proper full-height list and renders the border cleanly inside the clip so nothing gets cut.

Same as official:
Every element, theme, animation and method is the untouched official Gen2. If Sirius ships an update to Gen2, Better picks it up automatically, since it loads the live official source and only patches the two things above on top.
